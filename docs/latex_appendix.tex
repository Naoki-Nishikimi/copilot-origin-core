\section*{Appendix A: Prototype Algorithms}
\paragraph{Multi-layer Coordinates.}
We define semantic coordinates as a concatenation of surface, conceptual, and relational vectors:
\begin{align}
\mathbf{v}_{sem} = [\mathbf{v}_{surf} \, ; \, \mathbf{v}_{concept} \, ; \, \mathbf{v}_{rel}].
\end{align}
Surface embeddings are deterministic hash vectors; conceptual vectors derive from a lexicon of
conceptual triggers; relational vectors are hashed co-occurrence cues.

\paragraph{Temporal Layers.}
We encode four families of temporal signals:
absolute sinusoidal time, relative time-delta to the query, periodic (weekday/month),
and an era bucket:
\begin{align}
\mathbf{v}_{temp} = [\mathbf{v}_{abs} \, ; \, \mathbf{v}_{rel} \, ; \, \mathbf{v}_{per} \, ; \, \mathbf{v}_{era}].
\end{align}

\paragraph{Fusion and Retrieval.}
The prototype fuses by concatenation
$\mathbf{v} = [\mathbf{v}_{sem};\mathbf{v}_{temp}]$
and ranks documents via cosine similarity.
Production systems may replace concatenation with learned fusion and add
graph-based re-ranking consistent with Contract Tag constraints.
